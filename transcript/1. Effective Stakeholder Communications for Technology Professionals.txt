[Video description begins] Course title: Effective Stakeholder Communications for Technology Professionals. [Video description ends]

As a technology professional, you have to communicate with your project stakeholders on a regular basis, whether it's to update them on progress, alert them to changes, or warn them about potential problems. By communicating openly and effectively with project stakeholders, you'll earn their support and trust. And you'll raise the profile of the IT technology functions within the organization as you help them realize their own objectives. In this course, you will learn how to identify your project stakeholders, how to choose the right channels with which to communicate with them, and how to hone your stakeholder message. You'll also explore ways to establish trust with your stakeholders through better conveying difficult project communications, and how to engage an audience through storytelling.
